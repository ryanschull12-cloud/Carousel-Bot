## New entries

- Marketing-niche carousel structured as a "value escalation": hook, problem, six slides building value, then the single best insight lands mid-carousel (around slide 9 of 12) instead of right after the hook -- makes the reader feel like they earned it rather than got it handed over. Frame test worth borrowing: judge every hook/body slide against "would someone actually save this," not just whether it's punchy. Also used numbered "STEP" pill badges on body slides -- same idea as our topic badge, worth trying as a step counter for step-by-step format carousels.
