Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-24

Carousel Bot ("the App") is an internal automation tool built and operated for a single digital marketing agency. This policy explains what data the App handles.

Who this applies to

The App has no public users. It is not distributed to, installed by, or used by anyone outside the operating agency. It does not collect data from the general public, website visitors, or third parties.

What data is processed

The App processes only the following, all of which belong to the operating agency itself:

  • Content generated by the App (marketing carousel text and images)
  • API access tokens used to publish that content to the agency's own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram)
  • Basic engagement metrics (likes, comments, saves, shares, reach) returned by those platforms' APIs for posts the App itself published

No data belonging to any other TikTok user, account, or third party is accessed, stored, or processed.

Data storage

Generated content and engagement metrics are stored in the App's private automation infrastructure (a version-controlled repository and associated task runner) for the purpose of avoiding repeated content and improving future posts. Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in that same infrastructure and are not shared with any third party.

Data sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Data is only ever sent to the social media platforms (TikTok, Instagram) the App publishes to, using their own official APIs, for the sole purpose of publishing the agency's own content.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the repository owner via the contact details on the associated GitHub account.
