# Hook Bank — Calibration Reference (252 hooks)

This is a **style and length calibration reference**, not a copy-paste bank. It exists to show
the content brain exactly what "short, simple, warm, not harsh" looks like in practice, across
all 7 angles and all 3 niches. Every hook below is 4-9 words, plain everyday language, no jargon,
no accusation aimed at the reader ("you're failing at X") — just a plain, curious, or reassuring
observation.

Rules for using this file:
- Write NEW hooks in this shape and length every day — do not copy these verbatim into a live
  batch more than very occasionally, and never twice for the same account.
- Match the WARMTH: these hooks inform and reassure. None of them shame the reader.
- Match the LENGTH: most are under 8 words. That is the target, not the ceiling.
- Match the SIMPLICITY: a 12-year-old could read any line here on the first pass.

## Addendum — extra opener shapes (from external hook-formula research, adapted)

Ryan shared a general 250-hook swipe file (Hormozi/OpusClip/etc. style, sourced 2026-07-31).
Its formula categories are useful for STRUCTURE; most of its literal wording is not a fit for
this account, because it posts as a knowledgeable agency, not a personal creator. Two things
were filtered out before anything below was adapted:
- Shame/accusation wording ("you're broke," "you're doing this wrong," "stop scrolling!") —
  conflicts with SIMPLICITY & WARMTH BAR.
- Fabricated personal proof ("I made $40k from one DM," "I went from X to Y") — this account
  has no personal creator persona and must never invent a specific personal result.

What WAS worth borrowing: three opener shapes not already in the 7-angle system — question
hooks, comparison hooks, and direct-address hooks (see HOOK OPENER VARIETY in
content_brain_system_prompt.txt, shapes 7-9). Adapted examples, same 4-9 word / plain / warm
register as the rest of this file:

### Question openers
- Why do some Google Ads accounts pay double?
- Why does the same ad get shown differently?
- Why do some emails skip the inbox entirely?
- What actually decides who sees your ad?
- Why do two identical ads perform so differently?
- What happens the moment your budget resets?

### Comparison openers
- Broad match versus phrase match: real difference
- Manual bidding versus automatic: what changes
- A rested audience versus a tired one
- Old subscribers versus new ones: real gap
- Before the pause versus after: real numbers

### Identity/direct-address openers
- Running Google Ads? One setting matters most.
- Sending email campaigns? This habit protects you.
- Advertising on Meta? Timing beats budget here.
- Managing your own ads? Check this weekly.
- Building an email list? Size isn't the goal.

Categories intentionally NOT adapted (present in the source file, skipped here, do not use):
confession/personal-story hooks, authority-by-personal-experience hooks ("I've worked in X for Y
years"), urgency/scarcity countdowns ("only 3 spots left"), and gimmick openers ("POV:," "Don't
scroll!", "Nobody:... Me:"). None of these fit a brand-voice agency account posting educational
content, not a personal creator's growth content.
